Stay with us

A quiet place for slow living.

Slow mornings, reliable Starlink and calm evenings on a cliffside by the sea. If that sounds like your kind of stay, you will feel right at home here.

You'll love it here if

  • You want peace and quiet
  • You work remotely, or you're retired
  • You're travelling solo or as a couple, at any age
  • You love dogs
  • You'd like to stay weeks or months

The house rhythm

  • Quiet hours from 9pm to 7 or 8am, so everyone sleeps well
  • A smoke-free property, indoors and out
  • Music stays low by the pool, so everyone can hear the sea
  • Raise a glass, and keep the evenings easy
  • Kindness to other guests, our team and our neighbours
  • Mostly a calm, work-friendly place for adults. Coming with children? Ask us

Why we ask

Guests here join a small community with a rhythm.

That's why every long stay begins with a short, friendly chat. It's how we make sure the place you arrive at is the place you were hoping for.

Common questions

What's included in the monthly rate?

Your own cottage and everything in it, Starlink internet, plus the pool, the gazebo on the cliff and the grounds. Electricity is billed separately, by what you use, and the cleaning is yours, so the cottage runs like a home of your own.

How many people can stay in a cottage?

The 2 Story House, Gray House, Amakan 1 and Amakan 2 each sleep two in a queen bed. The Eco House and the Backpacker's House are made for one. Coming with more people? Ask us and we will see what we can arrange.
